}{
    {\bfseries{\textsf{convector}}} \scalebox{.8}\faGithub
} \hfill {\ralewaythin{\scriptsize{Python $\cdot$ Gensim}}}

\hfill\begin{minipage}[t]{0.8\textwidth}
\setlength{\parskip}{1em} \setlength{\baselineskip}{3ex}

\vspace{0ex}
Applies distributional semantics and semantic matching toward
an automated solver for the New York Times Games Connections puzzle.

Trains CBOW embeddings on Wikipedia and clusters the context vectors via
DBSCAN to identify distinct meanings
before grouping different words' senses by cosine similarity.

\end{minipage}

\urllink{
    https://github.com/\github/asofterworld
}{
    {\bfseries{\textsf{asofterworld}}} \scalebox{.8}\faGithub
} \hfill {\ralewaythin{\scriptsize{Python $\cdot$ OpenCV $\cdot$ BeautifulSoup}}}

\hfill\begin{minipage}[t]{0.8\textwidth}
\setlength{\parskip}{1em} \setlength{\baselineskip}{3ex}

\vspace{0ex}
Bridges syntactic theory and NLP to explore whether panel divisions in
the webcomic \textit{A Softer World} separate syntactic consituents.

It scrapes the webcomic's images then detects and partitions panels, assigning
OCR-extracted text according to panel boundaries, and passes that text to an
NLP pipeline.
\end{minipage}
